docs » ScreenRecorder

Record a selected area of the screen to .mov, converted to GIF, WebP, AVIF or MP4 when ffmpeg is installed

Download: https://github.com/Hammerspoon/Spoons/raw/master/Spoons/ScreenRecorder.spoon.zip
